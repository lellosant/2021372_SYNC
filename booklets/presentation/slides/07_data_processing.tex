\begin{frame}{Data Processing \& Deduplication}
The same physical location often appears multiple times in the ERP dataset (e.g. multiple invoice or delivery rows).

\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{block}{ERP Deduplication Example (Selected Company: WINES)}
\centering \footnotesize
Customer A -- Via Roma 10 -- Row 1 (WINES): EUR 600\\
Customer A -- Via Roma 10 -- Row 2 (WINES): EUR 400

\vspace{0.1cm}
$\downarrow$ \textbf{GROUP BY PHYSICAL LOCATION} $\downarrow$

\vspace{0.1cm}
\textbf{Customer A -- Via Roma 10 -- Total WINES: EUR 1,000} \textit{(1 visit planned)}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}
    \small
    \item \textbf{Flexible parser}: Handles arbitrary column order and discovers new companies.
    \item \textbf{Deduplicate}: Groups duplicate ERP rows for the chosen company.
    \item \textbf{Geocode}: Converts unique delivery addresses into coordinates.
    \item \textbf{Travel matrix}: Computes driving times with OSRM and Haversine.
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
