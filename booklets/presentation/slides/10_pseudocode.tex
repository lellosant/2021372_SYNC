\vspace{0.2cm}
\footnotesize
\textbf{DESTROY}\\
Remove some visits (random/cluster).

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{REPAIR}\\
Reinsert visits in optimal positions.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{ACCEPT}\\
Keep improving plans or explore with probability $p$.

\vspace{0.3cm}
\textbf{BEST}\\
Track the best plan found so far.
