\begin{frame}{Optimization Algorithm}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Nature of the Problem:} The routing involves maximizing visited revenue while respecting time limits (Orienteering Problem).
    \item \textbf{Approach:} We implemented a \textbf{heuristic optimization algorithm} (e.g., Adaptive Large Neighborhood Search - ALNS).
    \item \textbf{No Global Optimum Guarantee:} Due to the problem's complexity (NP-hard), the algorithm efficiently finds a high-quality, practical solution rather than a mathematically guaranteed global optimum.
    \item \textbf{Real-World Constraints:} 
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Weekends and Italian national holidays are excluded.
            \item Working hours, travel durations, and user-defined visit durations are strictly respected.
        \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
